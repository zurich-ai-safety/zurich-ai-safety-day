Making the most of Swiss AI Safety Days

Most of the value of Swiss AI Safety Days comes from the people you meet, so make connecting with others your number 1 priority. Don't try to attend every session: skipping a talk for a good conversation is often the better choice.

  • Networking: use Swapcard to request short 1:1s, and propose a time and place in your first message.
    • Open with: “Here's what I want to get out of this 1:1” and “What problem are you most stuck on right now?”
    • Close with “Who else here should I talk to?”
  • At the org fair: arrive with three organisations you want to talk to, and ask each for one concrete next step, such as a call next week or a task to try.
  • At the poster session: posters say how presenters can help others, or how others can help them. Look for the QR codes.
  • Before you leave: fill in the feedback survey at the closing session. It helps us improve next year's event, and filling it in is the single best thing you can do to help us.

Before you arrive

  • Swapcard: log in to Swapcard, our event platform, using the invitation we emailed you. Fill in your profile, browse the programme and start booking 1:1 meetings. Swapcard's AI assistant can suggest people to meet.
  • Your ticket: your ticket is in Luma. Have its QR code ready when you arrive.
  • Your badge: you'll get a name badge at registration. Please wear it throughout the event and bring it back on Sunday.
  • Participant-organised events: see the events other participants are organising, or add your own.
  • Can't come? Let us know at conferences@aisafety.ch as soon as possible, so someone on the waitlist can take your place.

Key information

Opening times

  • Saturday 7 November: doors open at 8:30 for registration, breakfast and speed networking (8:45 to 9:15). Sessions start at 9:30 sharp. The day ends with an apéro from 18:00.
  • Sunday 8 November: doors open at 8:30 for breakfast, and the opening session starts at 9:00. The closing session runs from 16:15 to 16:45.

Food

  • Included: breakfast, lunch, coffee and snacks on both days, and an apéro on Saturday evening.
  • Dietary requirements: All food is vegan. Allergen information is displayed next to all food. If you are unsure about a dish, ask a volunteer.
  • Water: please bring a reusable bottle if you can.

Venue and spaces

Please refer to the venue map to find your way around. Key spaces:

  • Main plenary room (HG E7): opening and closing sessions, keynotes, talks and panels.
  • Main hall (HG E4): the org fair on Saturday from 12:30 to 15:00, and the poster session on Sunday from 14:00 to 15:00.
  • Coat room (HG E41): unsupervised, so please keep valuables with you.
  • Silent work and rest room: HG E3, open all day for working quietly or resting.

Health, well-being and accessibility

  • If you have symptoms of illness (such as fever, cough or stomach problems) just before the event, please stay at home and let us know.
  • Feel free to wear a mask. Respect personal space, and ask before hugging or shaking hands.
  • The ETH main building is wheelchair accessible via the Rämistrasse entrance, and lifts connect all floors. If you need assistance, email us before the event so we can make arrangements.

Recordings, photos and media

  • Recordings: talks in E7 and E5 are recorded and published afterwards. Workshops, discussion groups and office hours are not recorded.
  • Photos: always ask before photographing or recording someone else.
  • Press: a small number of journalists will attend (by invitation only). Workshops, discussion groups and office hours are closed to press. Unless a speaker says otherwise, remarks in talks and panels may be quoted. See our press policy for the full rules.
